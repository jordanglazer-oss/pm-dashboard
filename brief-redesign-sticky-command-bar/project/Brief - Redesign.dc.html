Composite signal Neutral–Bullish · conviction moderate · stable 9 sessions {{ compositeCaret }}

Six of eleven regime signals read risk-on, three risk-off, two neutral — a composite stable for nine sessions. The risk-off contributors are all participation-based (50DMA breadth, equal-weight relative, advance/decline line) while every credit and cross-asset input remains benign. That is the classic late-advance signature rather than a topping one: narrow tapes can persist for months, but they raise the cost of being wrong on any single mega-cap print.

What the tape may be under-pricing non-consensus · September cut odds {{ underCaret }}

Rate-cut odds for September sit near 80% while core PCE has not confirmed. A hawkish hold on Wednesday would reprice the front end faster than equities are positioned for. The cheapest expression is short-duration quality rather than index protection — puts are not cheap enough to be the vehicle for a rates view.

Breadth & internals three straight weeks of deterioration {{ breadthCaret }}

The 50DMA measure has fallen in each of the last three weeks while the index made new highs — the single clearest negative on the board. New highs (86) still comfortably exceed new lows (41), so this is narrowing rather than distribution, but up-volume at 58% is well short of the 85% thrust reading that would confirm a broadening.

Credit & volatility no corroboration for a de-risk {{ creditCaret }}

Spreads sit inside the 20th percentile of the last decade and have not widened on a single day this month. The vol surface is fully normal: contango, VVIX 91, no bid for downside skew ahead of FOMC. When breadth deteriorates without credit confirming, the historical resolution is a shallow index pullback rather than a regime change.
